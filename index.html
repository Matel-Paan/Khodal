Shipping Information

Since all our products are custom-made and printed on demand, we ensure careful processing for every order. We partner with India's leading POD supplier to deliver premium quality to your doorstep.

Production Time

Every piece is printed exclusively for you after the order is placed. Please allow 2 to 4 business days for your order to be printed, quality-checked, and packed.

Delivery Time

Once dispatched, shipping usually takes:

  • Metro Cities: 3 to 5 business days
  • Rest of India: 5 to 7 business days

You will receive a tracking link via SMS once your order is shipped.

Returns & Exchanges

At TobaKart, every garment is custom-printed precisely to your specifications upon order. Due to the nature of Print-On-Demand manufacturing, we have a strict policy to ensure quality and sustainability.

No Size Exchanges or Buyer's Remorse

Because your item is uniquely made for you, we do not accept returns or process exchanges for incorrect size selection, buyer's remorse, or color preference changes. We strongly encourage reviewing our Size Guide carefully before placing your order.

Damaged or Defective Items

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you receive a product with a manufacturing defect, print error, or transit damage, we will gladly provide a free replacement. You must submit a request with clear photos of the defect within 7 days of receiving the product.

How to claim a replacement:

  • Contact our support team within 7 days of delivery.
  • Provide your Order ID and clear pictures showing the defect.
  • Once approved, a replacement will be printed and shipped at no additional cost.

About TobaKart

Welcome to TobaKart, where streetwear meets modern culture. We are an independent apparel brand focused on delivering premium, heavyweight garments with striking graphic designs.

Our Mission

To empower individual expression through high-quality, print-on-demand fashion. By utilizing a POD model, we eliminate mass overproduction and reduce fashion waste, ensuring that every garment created is one that was specifically desired by our community.
